1·What Happened

Two new AI tools have launched: Tao ChatGPT and Jacobian.

Tao ChatGPT is a version of ChatGPT specifically optimized for mathematical reasoning, with 30% higher accuracy on math competition problems than the standard version. Jacobian is a symbolic computation tool that can derive formulas, solve equations, and prove theorems.

The combination of these two tools means AI can not only "calculate" math but also "understand" it. That's a qualitative leap.

2·Why It Matters

This shows AI is surpassing humans in "mathematics" — a traditionally human-strong domain. Previously, math was considered a field requiring "creativity" where AI couldn't compete. Now AI doesn't just solve problems — it discovers new proof methods.

This has profound implications for education, research, and engineering. Mathematicians won't lose their jobs, but the way math is done will change.

More importantly: math is the foundation of science. If AI can "understand" math, it can "understand" physics, chemistry, biology. This means AI's potential in science is limitless.

💡 Think of It This Way

Math is like a language. AI has learned the "grammar" (computation) and is now understanding the "semantics" (reasoning). When AI can "understand" math, it can "understand" the underlying logic of science.

3·Practical Takeaways

✎
Use AI to assist with math work: If you need mathematical derivations, Tao ChatGPT or Jacobian can help. But always verify results.
↻
Don't fully rely on AI: AI is strong at math but not 100% accurate. Critical steps need human verification — especially safety-critical calculations.
⚒
Learn new math tools: Symbolic computation tools will keep getting more powerful. Learning to use them boosts efficiency. But more importantly: understand the math principles, not just the tools.

3. My Take

Mathematicians won't lose their jobs, but math work will change. AI handles "computation"; humans focus on "creation."

What is creation? Asking new questions, discovering new patterns, building new theories. These are things AI can't do. At least not yet.

My advice: don't fear AI's math capabilities. AI can calculate, but creation remains humanity's monopoly. Find your creativity — that's what AI can't replace.

Math is the foundation of science, but science isn't just math. Science also needs imagination, intuition, insight. These are uniquely human abilities.
